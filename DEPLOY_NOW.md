## Step 1: Save (If Not Already Saved)

If you haven't clicked "Save" yet:
1. Scroll down to the bottom
2. Click the black **"Save"** button
3. Wait for confirmation

---

## Step 2: Go to Deployments

1. Click on **"Deployments"** tab (top navigation)
2. You'll see a list of your deployments

---

## Step 3: Redeploy

1. Find the **latest deployment** (top of the list)
2. Click the **three-dot menu** (⋮) on the right
3. Click **"Redeploy"**
4. OR click directly on the deployment and click **"Redeploy"** button

---

## Step 4: Wait for Build

1. You'll see the build progress
2. Wait **1-2 minutes** for:
   - ✅ Installing dependencies
   - ✅ Building your app
   - ✅ Deploying to Vercel

---

## Step 5: Visit Your Live Site!

Once deployment shows **"Ready"**:

1. Click the **"Visit"** button (or the deployment URL)
2. Your site is now live! 🎉

---

## Step 6: Test Your Site

Check these:
- ✅ Homepage loads
- ✅ Projects page works
- ✅ Forms can be submitted
- ✅ API endpoints work

## Troubleshooting

### Build Fails?
- Check build logs in Vercel dashboard
- Verify `DATABASE_URL` format is correct
- Make sure all dependencies are in `package.json`

### Site Not Working?
- Check function logs: Vercel Dashboard → Functions tab
- Verify environment variables are saved
- Check deployment logs for errors
